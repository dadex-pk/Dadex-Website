Product Overview

Ducting that protects cable infrastructure.

PE Cable Duct is a dual-material ducting system available in polyethylene (PE) and unplasticised polyvinyl chloride (UPVC). PE ducts are supplied in long coils with a ribbed interior surface for cable-friendly routing, while UPVC ducts offer socket-ended pipe for solvent-cement or rubber-ring jointing.

Product at a glance

  • Durability
  • Superior resistance to corrosion, chemicals, weather & abrasion
  • Leak-proof joints, preventing ingress of groundwater
  • Easy to handle and install
