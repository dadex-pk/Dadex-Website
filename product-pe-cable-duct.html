Product Overview

Ducting that protects cable infrastructure.

PE Cable Duct is a versatile, dual-material ducting system available in polyethylene (PE) and unplasticised polyvinyl chloride (UPVC). PE ducts are supplied in long coils with a ribbed interior surface for cable-friendly routing, while UPVC ducts offer socket-ended pipe for solvent-cement or rubber-ring jointing.

Application Areas

Where PE Cable Duct can be used

  • Fibre optic cable protection
  • Electrical cable routing
  • Telecommunication networks
  • Underground utility crossings

Leak-proof joints prevent the ingress of groundwater, protecting sensitive cables.

Technical Information

Specifications & Product Range

Key technical characteristics and available dimensions of the Cable Duct system.

MaterialPolyethylene (PE) and unplasticised polyvinyl chloride (UPVC).
AppearanceUPVC ducts: Gray colour with one end of the pipe socket. PE ducts: Black with a ribbed interior surface.
Available RangeUPVC ducts: Diameters 40, 50, 75, and 110mm in lengths of 6m.

PE ducts: Diameters 25, 32, 50, and 90mm in coils of 100m.
Jointing MethodPVC-U ducts: Solvent cement or rubber ring joints. PE ducts: Compression fittings.
Installation

Proper installation for continuous protection.

Proper handling and jointing ensure the duct remains watertight and protects cables from mechanical damage and groundwater ingress.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure trench beds are free of sharp rocks.
  • Use appropriate draw ropes or mandrels to check duct alignment.
  • Keep duct ends sealed during installation to prevent debris entry.
  • Ensure adequate compaction around the duct during backfilling.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
