PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Ducting that protects cable infrastructure.

PE Cable Duct is a dual-material ducting system available in polyethylene (PE) and unplasticised polyvinyl chloride (UPVC). PE ducts are supplied in long coils with a ribbed interior surface for cable-friendly routing, while UPVC ducts offer socket-ended pipe for solvent-cement or rubber-ring jointing. Both are designed to protect fibre optics, electrical and communication cables in underground utility installations.

Technical Specifications

PE Cable Duct
MaterialPolyethylene (PE) and unplasticised polyvinyl chloride (UPVC).
AppearanceUPVC ducts: Gray colour with one end of the pipe socket. PE ducts: Black with a ribbed interior surface.
Standards & SpecificationsProduct dimensions and material grades are specified in the Dadex product booklet for this system. Confirm the applicable standard with Dadex Technical Support before specification.
Available RangeUPVC ducts: Diameters 40, 50, 75, and 110mm in lengths of 6m. PE ducts: Diameters 25, 32, 50, and 90mm in coils of 100m.
Jointing MethodPVC-U ducts: Solvent cement or rubber ring joints. PE ducts: Compression fittings.

Durability

Resistant to corrosion, chemicals, weather and abrasion for long service life in underground installations.

Leak-proof joints

Jointing methods are designed to prevent groundwater ingress into the ducting network.
