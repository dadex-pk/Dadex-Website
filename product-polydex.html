Product Overview

A practical piping solution for hot and cold water distribution.

Polydex is a polypropylene random copolymer (PP-R) piping system developed for domestic and commercial water supply installations. Its heat-fusion jointing method creates integrated pipe connections for distribution networks within buildings.

Application Areas

Where Polydex can be used

  • Domestic hot and cold water distribution
  • Residential buildings
  • Commercial buildings
  • Internal building water supply networks

Final application suitability should be assessed against the system's design conditions and applicable technical requirements.

Installation

Proper installation for dependable performance.

Correct installation practices are essential to the performance and service life of a piping system. Polydex connections are made using heat-fusion jointing.

Installation considerations

  • Use compatible system pipes and fittings.
  • Follow the applicable heat-fusion procedure.
  • Observe the required installation and operating conditions.
  • Carry out appropriate inspection and testing before commissioning.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
