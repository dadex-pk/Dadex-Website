Product Overview

Dependable PP-R piping for hot and cold water.

Polydex is a polypropylene random copolymer (PP-R) piping system for hot and cold water distribution in buildings. It offers a durable, economical alternative for domestic and commercial water supply applications, with heat-fusion jointing for leak-free connections.

Product at a glance

  • Resistance to corrosion & abrasion
  • Environment friendly
  • Welding capability
  • Low electrical & heat conductivity
