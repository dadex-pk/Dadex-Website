Product Overview

Dependable PP-R piping for essential water services.

Thermoline is a polypropylene random copolymer (PP-R) piping system designed for domestic and commercial hot and cold water distribution. It offers a durable, economical alternative for building services, featuring heat-fusion jointing for reliable, leak-free connections.

Application Areas

Where Thermoline can be used

  • Hot and cold water distribution in buildings
  • Residential and commercial sanitary systems
  • Areas prone to seismic activity
  • Food-grade applications

Its welding capability and material properties make it a versatile and safe choice for modern plumbing.

Installation

Proper installation for dependable performance.

Thermoline's lightweight nature and heat-fusion jointing method make it easy to handle, transport, and install, even in complex building layouts.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure the fusion tool is at the correct temperature.
  • Cut pipes squarely and clean surfaces before heating.
  • Allow adequate cooling time before handling fused joints.
  • Test the system thoroughly before commissioning.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
