Profile of Company

Dadex was established as a public limited company in 1959. The company began with fibre-cement roofing systems in Hyderabad under the leadership of Founder Chairman Kassim Dada. Under the leadership of Sikander Dada, the business diversified into plastic piping systems.

Today Dadex manufactures thermoplastic systems including UPVC, PP-R, PP-RCT, PP and PE. Headquartered in Karachi, the company operates manufacturing facilities in Hyderabad and Sundar Industrial Estate near Lahore.

Dadex operations are supported by ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certifications together with PSQCA certification. Its focus is customer service, quality, innovation and reliable technical support.

Permissible Business

The principal business of the Company is the manufacture and sale of construction material, mainly including piping systems and allied products manufactured from chrysotile fibre cement, rubber and plastics, together with merchandising of imported fittings, accessories and other building products.

Governance / Code of Conduct

Dadex's governance principles emphasise honesty, integrity, legal and regulatory compliance, fair competition, responsible business conduct, product safety, environmental responsibility, reliable financial reporting, merit-based employment, confidentiality and avoidance or disclosure of conflicts of interest.

  1. Conduct business honestly and ethically, respecting stakeholders.
  2. Comply with Pakistani laws and regulations.
  3. Support fair competition and comply with competition laws.
  4. Do not support political parties or political-party interests.
  5. Promote legitimate interests through trade associations.
  6. Provide safe products offering value in price and quality.
  7. Operate in an environmentally responsible manner and contribute to society.
  8. Maintain reliable financial reporting and transparency.
  9. Recruit and promote on merit and maintain safe working conditions.
  10. Protect company information and assets and disclose conflicts of interest.
  11. Board committees monitor compliance with these principles.
  12. Management is responsible for enforcement of laws and regulations.
  13. Do not facilitate arrangements involving proceeds of crime.
  14. Treat work information as confidential unless publicly available.

Shahzad M. Husain Chairman / Non-Executive Director

Mr. Shahzad M. Husain is a luminary in the realm of academia, holding the distinction of a Master's degree in Law and International Relations. His illustrious career is underscored by a rich tapestry of experiences and accomplishments. With two decades of dedicated service at Citibank, he exercised astute stewardship over Corporate and Wholesale Banking, exemplifying his acumen in the world of finance.

His footprint of influence extends beyond banking, as he has served as a pivotal figure on the boards of notable entities such as the Central Depository Company of Pakistan Limited, PICIC, and the esteemed Pakistan International Airlines.

Moreover, Mr. Shahzad M. Husain currently assumes the mantle of Chief Executive Officer at Muznash (Private) Limited, a consultancy firm that bears his imprimatur, established in 1998. This consultancy is renowned for its specialized prowess in the arena of financial advisory services, further underscoring his commitment to financial excellence.

In addition to his extensive portfolio of responsibilities, he lends his expertise as a Director on the distinguished board of Berger Paints Pakistan Limited, further enhancing the strategic landscape of this eminent organization.

Sikander Dada Chief Executive Officer / Executive Director

Mr. Sikander Dada occupies a prominent position within the corporate sphere, with a multifaceted journey that warrants attention. In the past year, he assumed the role of the Company's Chief Executive Officer, marking a significant milestone in his illustrious career. His tenure began in 1989 and extended until May 2014, at which point he held the esteemed position of Chairman on the Board of Directors of the Company. Furthermore, from 2022 onwards, he resumed the role of CEO, embarking on a new phase in his remarkable journey, all the while preserving and advancing the visionary legacy of the late Founder Chairman, Mr. Kassim Dada.

Mr. Dada's narrative of leadership spans an impressive 24-year span. During this time, the Company flourished under his guidance, experiencing consistent growth in sales volume, diversification of the product portfolio, incorporation of cutting-edge production technologies, and the strategic repositioning of Dadex as a beacon of innovation, unwavering dedication to customers, and an unwavering symbol of reliability in the industry.

Educationally, Mr. Sikander Dada is the holder of a distinguished B.Sc (Hons.) in Economics from the esteemed London School of Economics at the University of London. Prior to his association with the Company, he sought knowledge and expertise through a series of technical and management training programs conducted in Belgium, Holland, and the United Kingdom, thereby enriching his reservoir of skills and insights.

His commitment to international engagement is underscored by his foundational involvement in esteemed entities such as the Pak-Belgium Business Forum (PBBF), the Pak-France Business Alliance, and the Pakistan India Business Forum. Additionally, his standing as a Member of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) and the SAARC Chamber of Commerce further exemplifies his global outlook. Furthermore, his role as one of the founding Trustees of the Duke of Edinburgh's Award Foundation in Pakistan reflects his deep-seated dedication to fostering personal development and achievement among the youth.

Mr. Sikander Dada's influence within the corporate landscape extends to his pivotal role in igniting a revolution in plastic piping within Pakistan, a testament to his unwavering commitment to technological progress. Furthermore, his collaborative efforts with the government in formulating policies for high-efficiency irrigation systems stand as a testament to his dedication to promoting sustainable development.

In addition to these diverse responsibilities, Mr. Dada concurrently serves as the Managing Director of Sikander (Private) Limited. His expertise and influence further extend to his role as a Director on the Board of Berger DPI (Private) Limited and Berdex Construction Chemicals (Private) Limited. Notably, his legacy of leadership includes a prior chairmanship of the Board at Berger Paints Pakistan Limited, solidifying his profound impact on the corporate landscape.

Syed Shahid Ali Bukhari Independent Director

Syed Shahid Ali Bukhari served as an Independent Director on the Company's Board. In this capacity, he assumed the pivotal role of leading the Board Audit Committee. His professional journey encompasses diverse experiences in various sectors of trade and commerce.

Mr. Bukhari initiated his career by working with several firms engaged in General trading in Karachi, focusing on the importation of chemicals and tea. Impressively, he ventured into establishing a General trading company in Dubai, with a primary focus on the trade of organic fertilizers and media. Beyond these trading ventures, he also made significant contributions to the fields of commercial construction and poultry farming.

Educationally, Mr. Bukhari completed his A-Levels at Karachi Grammar Schools before embarking on a transformative journey. He commenced his professional path as an articled clerk at the C.A. firm Somers Baker in London, following the successful completion of his foundation course at the Middlesex Institute.

Mr. Bukhari's commitment to excellence is exemplified by his attainment of Certified Director status. His unwavering dedication to ongoing learning is further underscored by his participation in the Directors' Training Program, which he completed at The Executive Development Center (EDC) of The University of Lahore. Notably, The University of Lahore is recognized as one of the approved institutions by the SECP for providing Directors' Training Programs in Pakistan. This attests to his commitment to upholding the highest standards of corporate governance and directorship.

Mr. Bukhari has completed three consecutive terms as an Independent Director of the Company. He remains eligible to contest the forthcoming election of Directors as a Non-Executive Director at the 67th Annual General Meeting scheduled for 27 October 2026.
