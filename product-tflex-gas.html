PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Engineered for dependable gas distribution.

T-Flex Gas is a polyethylene piping system for gas distribution networks. Manufactured in PE 80 and PE 100 grades, it offers corrosion resistance, flexibility and durability, and is compatible with fusion jointing methods for leak-free connections. Colour coding supports clear identification on site.

01

Product at a glance

  • Corrosion resistant
  • Relatively light in weight
  • Flexible & low frictional resistance
  • Good absorption of impact loads, vibration & ground movement
