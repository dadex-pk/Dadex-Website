Product Overview

Engineered for dependable gas distribution.

T-Flex Gas is a robust polyethylene piping system designed for gas distribution networks. Manufactured in PE 80 and PE 100 grades, it offers exceptional corrosion resistance, flexibility, and durability. It is fully compatible with fusion jointing methods to ensure leak-free connections.

Application Areas

Where T-Flex Gas can be used

  • Natural gas distribution networks
  • Residential and commercial gas supply
  • Industrial gas pipelines
  • Underground installations

Absorbs impact loads, vibration, and ground movement effectively.

Installation

Proper installation for leak-free gas networks.

Because gas lines require absolute integrity, proper fusion jointing and testing are critical to the installation process.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure fusion equipment is calibrated and clean.
  • Follow the specific heating and cooling times for PE 80/PE 100.
  • Use tracer wires for underground installations to aid future location.
  • Conduct rigorous pressure/leak testing before commissioning.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
