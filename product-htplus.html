Product Overview

High-performance PP building drainage.

HTplus is a modern polypropylene (PP) soil, waste, and ventilation system designed for internal building drainage[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span)[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span). Built to withstand high-temperature wastewater discharges up to 95°C and aggressive chemical agents ranging from pH 2 to pH 12, it provides reliable, long-lasting performance across demanding residential, commercial, and institutional applications[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

Application Areas

Where HTplus can be used

  • Internal sanitary drainage networks[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span)
  • High-temperature soil and waste discharge systems (up to 95°C)[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span)
  • Chemically aggressive wastewater drainage (pH 2 to pH 12)[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)
  • Ventilation piping in residential and commercial buildings

The smooth inner bore resists scaling and encrustation, ensuring optimal hydraulic flow and minimal maintenance.

Installation

Fast, reliable push-fit assembly.

Equipped with factory-installed sealing rings and a printed centimetre scale, HTplus allows quick, clean, and watertight installation without the need for solvent cements or welding[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

Installation considerations

  • Use the integrated centimetre scale to measure and cut pipes squarely[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span).
  • Chamfer cut pipe ends and ensure sealing rings are clean and properly seated.
  • Apply approved lubricant to the spigot end and push fully into the socket, leaving a standard thermal expansion gap.
  • Secure pipes with appropriate brackets to accommodate thermal expansion and prevent sagging.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
