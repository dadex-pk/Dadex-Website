Product Overview

Reliable protection for building wiring.

Dadex Electrical Conduits are UPVC pipes used for routing and protecting electrical wiring in buildings. The smooth interior and uniform shape support easy cable pulling, while the material inherently resists heat, rust, chemical attack, and decomposition.

Application Areas

Where Electrical Conduits can be used

  • Concealed wiring in concrete slabs and walls
  • Underground electrical routing
  • Surface-mounted wiring protection
  • Residential and commercial buildings

Best results are achieved when concealed in concrete or used underground.

Installation

Proper installation for safe wiring.

Proper installation ensures cables can be pulled easily without damage and that the conduit provides continuous mechanical protection.

Installation considerations

  • Cap the ends of conduits during construction to prevent concrete/debris entry.
  • Avoid tight bends that could damage cable insulation during pulling.
  • Use appropriate jointing methods to ensure a smooth internal bore.
  • Secure conduits adequately to prevent movement.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
