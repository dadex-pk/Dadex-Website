Product Overview

Reliable protection for building wiring.

Electrical Conduits from Dadex are UPVC pipes used for routing and protecting electrical wiring in buildings. The smooth interior and uniform shape support easy cable pulling, while the material resists heat, rust, chemical attack and decomposition.

Product at a glance

  • Flexible and uniform in shape
  • Resistant to chemical reactions
  • Best results when concealed in concrete or used underground
  • Heat resistant, rust-proof and free from decomposition
