Safety, Health & Environment

Responsible operations across our facilities.

Dadex places priority on employee safety, community wellbeing and environmental responsibility across its manufacturing sites.

  • The Company places tremendous importance towards safety, health of its employees, and the environment as a whole.
  • Facilities are maintained in a manner that protects the environment.
  • Employees and installers are trained to recognize and reduce environmentally harmful practices.
  • Waste recycling, dust control and monitoring systems are operational at all production facilities.
