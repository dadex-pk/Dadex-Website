Product Overview

Underground drainage for infrastructure works.

Flow Line is a complete UPVC underground sewerage system designed for gravity drainage. It is purpose-built for infrastructure-scale applications including public sewerage networks, highway drainage, and site drainage. The smooth bore supports efficient flow and low bacterial growth.

Application Areas

Where Flow Line can be used

  • Public sewerage networks
  • Highway and road drainage
  • Industrial site drainage
  • Gravity-fed wastewater systems

The non-corrosive nature and high chemical resistance ensure a long service life in underground environments.

Installation

Proper installation for efficient gravity flow.

Correct trench preparation and jointing are essential to ensure a leak-free, self-cleaning gravity sewerage network.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure the trench bed provides continuous, uniform support.
  • Maintain accurate gradients for gravity flow.
  • Clean joints thoroughly before applying solvent cement or rubber rings.
  • Conduct leakage and deflection tests prior to backfilling.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
