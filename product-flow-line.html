PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Underground drainage for infrastructure works.

Flow Line is a UPVC underground sewerage system for gravity drainage. It is designed for infrastructure-scale applications including public sewerage networks, highway drainage and site drainage. The smooth bore supports efficient flow and low bacterial growth.

Product at a glance

  • Non-corrosiveness & high chemical resistance
