Product Overview

Protection against unwanted backflow.

Non-return valves are used on non-pressure sewerage and drainage lines to help prevent wastewater from flowing back through the drainage system. The valve closes automatically when sewage flows back, and the operating lever permits normal drainage operation and emergency closure.

Application Areas

Where Non-Return Valves are used

  • Non-pressure sewerage lines
  • Flood prevention in basements
  • Drainage pipe systems
  • Emergency flow isolation

For non-pressure sewage applications; not intended for cooling or heating equipment.

Installation

Proper installation for reliable protection.

Correct installation orientation is critical to ensure the automatic closure mechanism works effectively against backflow.

Installation considerations

  • Install with the flow direction arrow pointing towards the drainage outlet.
  • Ensure the operating mechanism is accessible for maintenance.
  • Keep the valve free of debris during installation.
  • Check that the valve seals correctly after installation.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
