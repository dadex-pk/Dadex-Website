PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Protection against unwanted backflow.

Non-return valves are used on non-pressure sewerage and drainage lines to help prevent wastewater from flowing back through the drainage system.

01

Product at a glance

  • For non-pressure sewerage applications
  • Automatic closure against backflow
  • Suitable for drainage pipe systems
  • Available in DN 110 and DN 160

Technical Specifications

Non-Return Valves
ProductNon-return valve for non-pressure sewerage and drainage systems.
Available sizesDN 110 and DN 160.
Intended useInstallation on sewerage drain pipes to help prevent wastewater backflow.
Maximum working pressure0.5 bar.
OperationThe valve closes automatically when sewage flows back. The operating lever permits normal drainage operation and emergency closure.
Important limitationFor non-pressure sewage applications; not intended for cooling or heating equipment.

Backflow protection

The valve is designed to close automatically when wastewater attempts to flow back through the drain line.

Operating access

The operating mechanism allows the valve to be managed according to the approved installation and maintenance procedure.
