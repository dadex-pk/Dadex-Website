The Dadex Story

A journey of trust from a vision to industry leadership. Since 1959, Dadex has grown from a pioneering manufacturer into a diversified provider of piping, drainage, and utility solutions.

Who we are

Over six decades of
engineering experience.

Dadex Eternit Limited was incorporated as a public limited company in 1959 and is listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange. The company manufactures and sells construction materials, principally piping systems and allied products, and also undertakes the merchandising of imported pipe fittings, accessories and other building products.

A pioneer of thermoplastic piping systems in Pakistan, Dadex has expanded from its early fibre cement roots into a broad portfolio serving construction, housing, architecture, telecommunications and infrastructure applications.

Founder Chairman

Kassim Dada (1919–2001)

A legacy of integrity, fairness and the sanctity of promise.

Kassim Dada, the Founder Chairman of Dadex, was a man of principles — an astute businessman, a philanthropist, an avid traveller and a man committed to basic values. His individuality influenced many to add meaning and significance to their own lives.

Following the simple principle of fairness, clarity and sincere application to all things in life, he worked to build a company recognised not only for the quality of its work and products, but for its integrity.
