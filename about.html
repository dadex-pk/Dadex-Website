Who we are

Over six decades of engineering experience.

Dadex Eternit Limited was incorporated as a public limited company in 1959 and is listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange. The company manufactures and sells construction materials, principally piping systems and allied products, and also undertakes the merchandising of imported pipe fittings, accessories and other building products.

A pioneer of thermoplastic piping systems in Pakistan, Dadex has expanded from its early fibre cement roots into a broad portfolio serving construction, housing, architecture, telecommunications and infrastructure applications.

Founder Chairman

Kassim Dada

A legacy of integrity, fairness and the sanctity of promise.

Kassim Dada (1919–2001), the Founder Chairman of Dadex, was an individual of principles — an astute businessman, a philanthropist, an avid traveller, and a man committed to basic values. His individuality influenced many to add meaning and significance to their own lives.

Following the simple principle of fairness, clarity and sincere application to all things in life, he worked hard to make Dadex a company recognised not only for the quality of its work and products, but also for its integrity.

“...the conduct towards others must be based on kindness, service, fairness, charity, justice, honesty and the sanctity of promise, pledge or agreement.” — A Ramble through Life, autobiography of Kassim Dada

The Dadex Vision reflects these qualities — the conviction that the strength and longevity of a company does not merely lie in the superiority of its products, but in how it conducts business. That principle remains at the heart of how Dadex serves customers, ensures product quality and builds long-term relationships.
