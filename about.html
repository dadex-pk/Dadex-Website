About Dadex

Since 1959, Dadex has evolved from a Pakistani manufacturing pioneer into a diversified construction-products company serving piping, drainage, utility and related building applications.

Who we are

Six decades of engineering experience.

Dadex Eternit Limited was incorporated as a public limited company on 13 April 1959 and is listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange. The company manufactures and sells construction materials, principally piping systems and allied products, and also undertakes the merchandising of imported pipe fittings, accessories and other building products.

Over the decades, Dadex has expanded from its early manufacturing roots into thermoplastic piping and infrastructure systems serving construction, housing, architecture, telecommunications and infrastructure applications.

Founder Chairman

Kassim Dada

A legacy of integrity, fairness and the sanctity of promise.

Kassim Dada (1919–2001), the Founder Chairman of Dadex, was an individual of principles — an astute businessman, philanthropist and a man committed to basic values.

Following the simple principle of fairness, clarity and sincere application to all things in life, he worked hard to make Dadex a company recognized not only for the quality of its work and products, but also for its integrity.

“...the conduct towards others must be based on kindness, service, fairness, charity, justice, honesty and the sanctity of promise, pledge or agreement.”

His values remain part of the legacy that shapes Dadex's approach to business, relationships and responsible conduct.
