Product Overview

Quiet drainage for high-quality buildings.

Polyduct is a low-noise soil, waste and ventilation system made from a proprietary blend of polypropylene (PP-MD) and mineral additives. The multi-layer construction reduces flow noise, resists high temperatures and resists chemical attack.

Product at a glance

  • Noise reduction: as low as 16 dB at a flow rate of 4 l/s
  • Temperature resistance: –20 °C to +95 °C
  • Chemical resistance: suitable for pH 2 to 12
  • Stiffness class: 2 SN8 kN/m² for diameters DN 110 to DN 200
