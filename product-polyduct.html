Product Overview

Quiet drainage for high-quality buildings.

Polyduct is a low-noise soil, waste, and ventilation system made from a proprietary blend of polypropylene (PP-MD) and mineral additives. Its multi-layer construction reduces flow noise, resists high temperatures, and resists chemical attack, making it ideal for premium residential and commercial developments.

Application Areas

Where Polyduct can be used

  • High-end residential drainage
  • Hotels, hospitals, and commercial buildings
  • Areas requiring strict noise control
  • Hot water discharge networks

Offers a noise reduction as low as 16 dB at a flow rate of 4 l/s.

Installation

Proper installation for optimal acoustic performance.

To maximize the noise-reduction benefits, careful attention must be paid to pipe clamping and routing to minimize structural vibration.

Installation considerations

  • Use appropriate acoustic pipe clips to isolate vibration.
  • Ensure lip seals are clean and properly lubricated.
  • Allow for thermal expansion, especially in hot water lines.
  • Avoid rigidly fixing pipes to lightweight walls where possible.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
