OUR HISTORY

Sixty-Seven Years of Excellence

A journey of trust from a vision to the industry leader. From a fibre cement plant in Hyderabad in 1959 to a diversified portfolio of piping, drainage, utility and roofing systems, Dadex has grown through product innovation, technical collaboration and a long-standing commitment to quality.

1959 — 2025

A journey in four eras.

Our history is a story of manufacturing, engineering partnerships and products developed for the changing needs of Pakistan's built environment.

The timeline below follows the historical milestones documented in Dadex's 67th Annual Report (2025–26).

Formative Years Resilience & Renewal Building Leadership Forging the Future

Dadex historical timeline

1959 – 1975

Formative Years

Building foundations with resilience and vision.

1959

  • Founded as a Public Limited Company

    Dadex is founded as a public limited company and listed on the Karachi Stock Exchange.

  • First Fibre Cement Plant

    Opening of the first fibre cement (FC) plant in Hyderabad.

  • FC Corrugated Sheets

    Launch of fibre cement corrugated roofing sheets.

  • FC Decorative Sheets

    Launch of fibre cement decorative sheets.

1962

Eternit Collaboration

Collaboration with the Eternit group of Belgium.

1964

FC Building Pipes

Launch of fibre cement building pipes.

1965

Chittagong Plant

Plant opens in Chittagong, East Pakistan. The plant was lost in 1971 following the independence of Bangladesh.

1966

  • Karachi Plant

    Plant opens in Karachi.

  • FC Pressure & Sewer Pipes

    Launch of fibre cement pressure pipes and fibre cement sewer pipes.

1967

ACIL Logo

Creation of the ACIL logo.

1976 – 1989

Resilience & Renewal

A period of resilience, progress and leadership transition.

1981

ACIL Sheets & Pipes

Expansion of the ACIL fibre cement sheets and pipes business.

1984

ACIL Cottage

ACIL Cottage is presented at an international exhibition.

1987

Pakistan Standards License

First Pakistan Standards License for fibre cement sheets received from PSI.

1989

Leadership Transition

Mr. Sikander Dada is appointed Chief Executive and Managing Director.

1990 – 2005

Building Leadership

Expanding horizons, strengthening capabilities, and leading the market.

1990

New Name, New Logo

Dadex adopts a new name and visual identity.

1991

  • Wavin Technical Collaboration

    Technical collaboration with Wavin, Netherlands, for the production of thermoplastic piping systems in Pakistan.

  • UPVC Pressure Pipes

    Launch of the UPVC pressure piping system.

1993

PE Pipes for Water & Gas

Launch of PE pipes for water and gas applications, including the T-Flex range.

1997

Nikasi

Launch of Nikasi, the UPVC SWV piping system.

1998

ISO 9000 Certification

Dadex achieves ISO 9000 certification.

1999

Polydex

Launch of Polydex, the PPR hot and cold water piping system.

2001

Dadex House

Dadex House is established on Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi.

2002

PE Cable Duct & UPVC Electrical Conduits

Launch of PE Cable Duct and UPVC Electrical Conduits.

2003

  • New Name, New Identity

    Dadex introduces a new name and identity.

  • Values, Vision & Mission

    Dadex establishes its Values, Vision and Mission statements.

2004

Zarkaasht

Launch of Zarkaasht, the high-efficiency irrigation system.

2005

  • American Petroleum Institute Certification

    The American Petroleum Institute certifies Dadex to use its official monogram on manufactured PE gas pipes. Dadex is the only company outside the US to achieve this distinction.

  • Investor in People

    Dadex is certified as an Investor in People (IIP) company on meeting the best HR practices and standards.

  • Aluminium Composite Panels

    Launch of Aluminium Composite Panels (ACP).

2006 – 2025

Forging the Future

Defining excellence through innovation, engineering and global standards.

2006

  • Flowline

    Launch of Flowline, the complete underground UPVC sewer pipe system.

  • UPVC Tubewell Casing & Screen Pipes

    Launch of the UPVC Tubewell Casing & Screen Pipes system.

  • SAP Solutions

    Dadex embraces SAP solutions.

2007

Lahore Plant

Plant opens in Lahore.

2008

Speciality Piping Systems

Launch of speciality piping systems.

2015

  • ECO SWV System

    Launch of ECO, the UPVC SWV piping system.

  • Thermoline

    Launch of Thermoline, the PPR hot and cold water piping system.

2016

Large-Diameter HDPE

Launch of large-diameter HDPE pipes up to 630 mm.

2017

Pakistan's First Antimicrobial Pipes

Launch of Pakistan's first antimicrobial pipes.

2018

  • Marley Plumbing & Drainage, UK

    Technical collaboration with Marley Plumbing and Drainage, United Kingdom.

  • Effast, Italy

    Collaboration with Effast, Italy, for UPVC pressure piping systems.

2021

Polydex Premium PP-RCT

Launch of Polydex Premium, the PP-RCT hot and cold water piping system.

2022

Magnaplast Collaboration

Collaboration with Magnaplast, Poland.

2023

Low-Noise SWV Piping System

Launch of the low-noise SWV piping system.

2024

Non-Return Valves

Launch of Non-Return Valves.

2025

Catch Basins

Launch of Catch Basins.

A LEGACY THAT CONTINUES

Across the decades, Dadex has expanded from fibre cement manufacturing into a broad portfolio of piping, drainage, ducting and roofing solutions, supported by technical capability and long-term customer relationships.
