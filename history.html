1959 – 1975

Formative Years

Building foundations with resilience and vision.

1959

  • Founded as a Public Limited Company

    Dadex is founded as a public limited company and listed on the Karachi Stock Exchange.

  • First Fibre Cement Plant

    Opening of the first fibre cement (FC) plant in Hyderabad.

  • FC Corrugated Sheets

    Launch of fibre cement corrugated roofing sheets.

  • FC Decorative Sheets

    Launch of fibre cement decorative sheets.

1962

Eternit Collaboration

Collaboration with the Eternit group of Belgium.

1964

FC Building Pipes

Launch of fibre cement building pipes.

1965

Chittagong Plant

Plant opens in Chittagong, East Pakistan. The plant was lost in 1971 following the independence of Bangladesh.

1976 – 1989

Resilience & Renewal

A period of resilience, progress and leadership transition.

1981

ACIL Sheets & Pipes

Expansion of the ACIL fibre cement sheets and pipes business.

1989

Leadership Transition

Mr. Sikander Dada is appointed Chief Executive and Managing Director.

1990 – 2005

Building Leadership

Expanding horizons, strengthening capabilities, and leading the market.

1991

  • Wavin Technical Collaboration

    Technical collaboration with Wavin, Netherlands, for the production of thermoplastic piping systems in Pakistan.

  • UPVC Pressure Pipes

    Launch of the UPVC pressure piping system.

1997

Nikasi

Launch of Nikasi, the UPVC SWV piping system.

2005

  • American Petroleum Institute Certification

    The American Petroleum Institute certifies Dadex to use its official monogram on manufactured PE gas pipes.

  • Investor in People

    Dadex is certified as an Investor in People (IIP) company.

2006 – 2025

Forging the Future

Defining excellence through innovation, engineering and global standards.

2006

  • Flowline

    Launch of Flowline, the complete underground UPVC sewer pipe system.

  • SAP Solutions

    Dadex embraces SAP solutions.

2017

Pakistan's First Antimicrobial Pipes

Launch of Pakistan's first antimicrobial pipes.

2021

Polydex Premium PP-RCT

Launch of Polydex Premium, the PP-RCT hot and cold water piping system.

2025

Catch Basins

Launch of Catch Basins.

A LEGACY THAT CONTINUES

From materials to complete systems.

Across the decades, Dadex has expanded from fibre cement manufacturing into a broad portfolio of piping, drainage, ducting and roofing solutions, supported by technical capability and long-term customer relationships.
