OUR HISTORY

Six Decades of Engineering Progress

From a fibre cement plant in Hyderabad in 1959 to a diversified portfolio of piping, drainage, utility and roofing systems, Dadex has grown through product innovation, technical collaboration and a long-standing commitment to quality.

1959 — PRESENT

A journey built milestone by milestone.

Our history is a story of manufacturing, engineering partnerships and products developed for the changing needs of Pakistan's built environment.

The timeline below follows the historical milestones documented in Dadex's corporate history material.

1959–1969 1980s 1990s 2000s 2010s 2020s

Dadex historical timeline

1959

  • Dadex Begins in Hyderabad

    Opening of Dadex's fibre cement plant in Hyderabad.

  • FC Decorative & Corrugated Sheets

    Dadex launches fibre cement decorative and corrugated sheets.

1962

Eternit Collaboration

Collaboration with the Eternit group of Belgium.

1964

FC Building Pipes

Launch of fibre cement building pipes.

1965

Chittagong Plant

Plant opens in Chittagong, East Pakistan. The plant was lost in 1971 following the independence of Bangladesh.

1966

Karachi Plant

Plant opens in Karachi.

1967

ACIL Identity

Creation of the ACIL logo.

1981

ACIL Sheets & Pipes

Expansion of the ACIL sheets and pipes business.

1984

ACIL Cottage

ACIL Cottage is presented at an international exhibition.

1987

Pakistan Standard License

First Pakistan Standard license for fibre cement sheets received from PSI.

1989

New Leadership

Mr. Sikander Dada is appointed Chief Executive and Managing Director.

1990

New Name, New Logo

Dadex adopts a new name and visual identity.

1991

  • Wavin Technical Collaboration

    Technical collaboration with Wavin, Netherlands, for the production of thermoplastic piping systems in Pakistan.

  • UPVC Pressure Pipes

    Launch of the UPVC pressure piping system.

1993

PE Pipes for Water & Gas

Launch of PE pipes for water and gas applications, including the T-Flex range.

1997

Nikasi

Launch of Nikasi, the UPVC SWV piping system.

1998

ISO Certification

Dadex achieves ISO certification.

1999

Polydex

Launch of Polydex, the PPR hot and cold water piping system.

2001

Dadex House

Dadex House is established on Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi.

2002

PE Cable Duct & UPVC Electrical Conduits

Launch of PE Cable Duct and UPVC Electrical Conduits.

2003

Values, Vision & Mission

Dadex establishes its Values, Vision and Mission statements.

2004

New Name, New Identity

Dadex introduces a new name and identity.

2005

  • American Petroleum Institute Certification

    The American Petroleum Institute certifies Dadex to use its official monogram on manufactured PE gas pipes. Dadex is the only company outside the US to achieve this distinction.

  • Investor in People

    Dadex is certified as an Investor in People (IIP) company on meeting the best HR practices and standards.

  • Aluminium Composite Panels

    Launch of Aluminium Composite Panels (ACP).

2006

  • Flowline

    Launch of Flowline, the complete underground UPVC sewer pipe system.

  • UPVC Tubewell Casing & Screen Pipes

    Launch of the UPVC Tubewell Casing & Screen Pipes system.

  • SAP Solutions

    Dadex embraces SAP solutions.

2007

Lahore Plant

Plant opens in Lahore.

2008

Speciality Piping Systems

Launch of speciality piping systems.

2015

ECO & Thermoline

Launch of ECO, the UPVC SWV piping system, and Thermoline, the PPR hot and cold water piping system.

2016

Large-Diameter PE

Launch of large-diameter PE pipes up to 630 mm.

2017

Pakistan's First Antimicrobial Pipes

Launch of Pakistan's first antimicrobial pipes.

2018

International Collaborations

Technical collaboration with Marley Plumbing and Drainage, UK, and collaboration with Effast, Italy, for UPVC pressure piping systems.

2021

Polydex PP-RCT

Launch of the Polydex PP-RCT piping system.

2023

Magnaplast Collaboration

Collaboration with Magnaplast, Poland.

2024

UltraDB Low-Noise SWV

Launch of the UltraDB low-noise SWV system.

A LEGACY THAT CONTINUES

From materials to complete systems.

Across the decades, Dadex has expanded from fibre cement manufacturing into a broad portfolio of piping, drainage, ducting and roofing solutions, supported by technical capability and long-term customer relationships.
