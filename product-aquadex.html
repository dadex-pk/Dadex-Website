Reliable pressure piping for potable water.

Aquadex pressure pipe system is a permanent solution for the main potable water supply pipeline. Aquadex pipes are safe for cold potable water distribution and transmission systems and can also be used for transportation of certain chemicals.

Why Aquadex?

  • Resistance to corrosion, abrasion and algae/fungi growth
  • Non-toxic and non-conductive
  • Lightweight, durable and economical
  • Smooth bore and simple joining
