Product Overview

Reliable pressure piping for potable water.

Aquadex is a robust UPVC pressure pipe system designed as a permanent solution for main potable water supply pipelines. It is entirely safe for cold potable water distribution and transmission, and can also be utilised for the transportation of certain chemicals.

Application Areas

Where Aquadex can be used

  • Main potable water supply networks
  • Water distribution and transmission
  • Infrastructure and municipal projects
  • Safe transportation of specific chemicals

Aquadex pipes and fittings are manufactured against rigorous standards to ensure safe, long-term performance.

Installation

Proper installation for dependable performance.

Correct installation practices are essential to the performance and service life of a piping system. Aquadex offers straightforward installation with solvent cement and rubber-ring Z-jointing options.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure pipes are cut squarely and cleanly.
  • Use appropriate solvent cement or Z-joint rubber rings.
  • Observe the required installation and operating conditions.
  • Carry out appropriate pressure testing before commissioning.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
