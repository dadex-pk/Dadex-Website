Product Overview

Dadex UPVC casing & screen pipes.

The heart of any water well is the casing pipe and screen. Properly selected and installed, they ensure that the well remains a perennial source of clean water. UPVC casing and screen pipes score tremendously over conventional metal piping because they are tough, do not corrode, and last for years.

Application Areas

Where Tubewell Pipes are used

  • Deep water well construction
  • Agricultural irrigation wells
  • Municipal water supply wells
  • Industrial boreholes

Economical, lightweight, and highly resistant to corrosion in subterranean environments.

Technical Information

Specifications & Product Range

Key technical characteristics and available dimensions of the Tubewell system.

Casing PipesUPVC casing pipes with male and female threaded sockets in diameters 4″ & 6″ (Class D). 8″ & 10″ (Class C & D) are available in a standard length of 4 meters.
Screen PipesUPVC screen pipes in diameters 4″ & 6″ (Class D). 8″ & 10″ (Class C & D) are available in a standard length of 4 meters.
Selection ConsiderationsCasing diameter, resistance to external/internal pressure, and design of slots.
Casing DiameterDadex UPVC casing pipes can be installed to depths of normal water well construction and in boreholes ranging from 4″, 6″, 8″ & 10″ in diameter. Internal diameter of the casing should ideally be at least 50mm (2″) greater than the outer diameter of the pump.
Pressure ResistanceMaximum hydraulic loading occurs during installation, gravel packing, backfilling and development. Dadex screen pipes have the capacity to withstand hydraulic pressure.
Installation

Proper installation for a long-lasting well.

Each installation must be assessed against site requirements before selecting the casing and screen pipe range.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure the borehole diameter allows for proper gravel packing.
  • Use appropriate threaded jointing techniques and sealants.
  • Centralize the casing string during installation.
  • Select the correct slot size for the surrounding aquifer material.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
