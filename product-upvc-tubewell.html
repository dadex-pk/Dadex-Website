Product Information

Dadex UPVC casing & screen pipes.

The heart of any water well is the casing pipe and screen. Properly selected and installed, they ensure that the water well remains a perennial source of clean water. UPVC casing pipes and screen pipes score tremendously over conventional metal piping because they are tough, do not corrode and last for years.

Advantages

  • Corrosion resistant
  • Lightweight
  • Easy to handle and install
  • Non-conductive
  • Economical

Specifications & range

Casing Diameters4″ to 10″
ClassesClass C & D
Standard Length4 metres
JointingThreaded sockets
Casing PipesUPVC casing pipes with male and female threaded sockets in diameters 4″ & 6″ (Class D). 8″ & 10″ (Class C & D) are available in a standard length of 4 meters.
Screen PipesUPVC screen pipes in diameters 4″ & 6″ (Class D). 8″ & 10″ (Class C & D) are available in a standard length of 4 meters.
Selection ConsiderationsCasing diameter, resistance to external/internal pressure, and design of slots.
Casing DiameterDadex UPVC casing pipes can be installed to depths of normal water well construction and in boreholes ranging from 4″, 6″, 8″ & 10″ in diameter. Internal diameter of the casing should ideally be at least 50mm (2″) greater than the outer diameter of the pump.
Pressure ResistanceMaximum hydraulic loading occurs during installation, gravel packing, backfilling and development. Dadex screen pipes have the capacity to withstand hydraulic pressure.

Corrosion resistance

UPVC casing and screen pipes do not corrode and are designed for durable water well service.

Threaded joints

Male and female threaded sockets support site assembly and installation.

Site assessment

Each installation should be assessed against site requirements before selecting the casing and screen pipe range.
