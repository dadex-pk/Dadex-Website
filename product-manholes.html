Product Overview

A complete system for underground sewer access.

The manhole system is built from compatible chamber bases, rising pipes, seals, covers and supporting components to provide access to underground sewerage and drainage networks. Components are designed as a coordinated system so that installation and configuration remain straightforward.

Product at a glance

  • Three system types: 315, 400 and 425
  • Complete modular chamber components
  • Multiple cover and frame configurations
  • Designed for sewerage and drainage infrastructure
