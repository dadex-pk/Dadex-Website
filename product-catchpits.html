Product Overview

Efficient rainwater and surface drainage.

Dadex Catchpits provide a practical, lightweight alternative to traditional concrete catch basins for rainwater and surface-water drainage. The system uses durable polypropylene construction, reinforced sides, and shaped pipe-entry points to simplify handling and connection on site.

Application Areas

Where Catchpits can be used

  • Rainwater collection points
  • Surface water drainage
  • Residential and commercial paving
  • Areas requiring lightweight installation

Technical Information

Specifications & Product Range

Key technical characteristics and available dimensions of the Catchpit system.

MaterialPolypropylene (PP), selected for its low weight, durability and resistance to typical drainage environments.
Available SizesSquare catchpit configurations are available across a range of sizes, including 200 × 200 mm, 300 × 300 mm, 400 × 400 mm and larger configurations up to approximately 550 × 550 mm, subject to the approved Dadex range.
Pipe ConnectionsSide openings use pre-cut concentric profiles so pipes of different sizes can be connected efficiently and accurately.
Structural DesignReinforced and ribbed side walls provide high resistance while keeping the unit lightweight for transport, positioning and installation.
Covers & GratingsCompatible PP covers and gratings can be used according to the installation and load requirements.
ApplicationSuitable for rainwater collection, surface drainage and other drainage installations.
Installation

Proper installation for efficient collection.

Lightweight construction allows for convenient handling and rapid installation, often requiring only a single person for suitable unit sizes.

Installation considerations

  • Excavate and level the pit area appropriately.
  • Use the pre-cut concentric side openings to connect pipes.
  • Backfill carefully to avoid distorting the chamber walls.
  • Fit the appropriate PP cover or grating for the surface load.

Final dimensions, cover classes and installation requirements should be verified against approved Dadex documentation.
