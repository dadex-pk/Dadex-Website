Product Overview

Efficient rainwater and surface drainage.

Catchpits provide a practical, lightweight alternative to traditional concrete catch basins for rainwater and surface-water drainage. The system uses polypropylene construction, reinforced sides and shaped pipe-entry points to simplify handling and connection on site.

Product at a glance

  • Polypropylene construction
  • Lightweight for easy handling and installation
  • Reinforced sides for high resistance
  • Pre-cut concentric side openings for pipe connections
  • Compatible covers and gratings

Specifications & range

MaterialPP (Polypropylene)

ConstructionReinforced side walls
ConnectionPre-cut inlets
MaterialPolypropylene (PP), selected for its low weight, durability and resistance to typical drainage environments.
Available sizesSquare catchpit configurations are available across a range of sizes, including 200 × 200 mm, 300 × 300 mm, 400 × 400 mm and larger configurations up to approximately 550 × 550 mm, subject to the approved Dadex range.
Pipe connectionsSide openings use pre-cut concentric profiles so pipes of different sizes can be connected efficiently and accurately.
Structural designReinforced and ribbed side walls provide high resistance while keeping the unit lightweight for transport, positioning and installation.
Covers & gratingsCompatible PP covers and gratings can be used according to the installation and load requirements.
InstallationLightweight construction allows convenient handling and installation, including positioning by a single person for suitable units.
ApplicationSuitable for rainwater collection, surface drainage and other drainage installations where a lightweight alternative to traditional concrete catch basins is required.
