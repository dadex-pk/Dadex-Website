Product Overview

Electroduct — safely wired.

Electroduct is engineered for buried electrical conduit applications where there is zero room for non-standard products. It offers safe usage of electricity with uniformity, flexibility, and complete technical backup from Dadex at all stages of the project.

Application Areas

Where Electroduct can be used

  • Buried electrical conduit applications
  • Concealed in concrete installations
  • Infrastructure and utility networks
  • Underground cable protection

Best results are achieved in "concealed in concrete" or underground applications.

Installation

Proper installation for reliable buried protection.

Manufactured to EN 50086, Electroduct requires compatible fittings to ensure the integrity of the buried installation.

Installation considerations

  • Use only compatible IEC 423 / EN 50086 fittings.
  • Ensure tight joints to prevent concrete ingress during pouring.
  • Provide adequate support in trenches to prevent sagging.
  • Use mandrels to verify clear pathways before pulling cables.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
