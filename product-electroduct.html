Product Overview

Electroduct — safely wired.

Electroduct is the first UPVC Electrical Conduit System in Pakistan manufactured as per the latest relevant international standards. It is meant for buried electrical conduit applications and is intended for projects where there is no room for non-standard products. It comes with full technical backup from Dadex at all stages.

Key features & benefits

  • Safe usage of electricity
  • Uniformity & flexibility
  • Best results in "concealed in concrete" or underground applications
  • Corrosion resistance
  • Smooth internal & external surface
