Product Overview

Corrugated sheets for roofing and wall cladding.

Dadex Corrugated Sheets are used for a wide variety of roofing and wall cladding applications. They facilitate design flexibility, reduce construction time, and increase the efficiency and quality of installation work, thereby resulting in lower project costs. The installation is especially simplified as the sheets do not require wet work and curing during construction.

Application Areas

Where Corrugated Sheets are used

  • Domestic and low-cost housing
  • Port structures
  • Industrial, agricultural and commercial buildings
  • Farms, sheds, go downs, warehouses and factories

Provides a fast, dry construction method with excellent weather resistance.

Installation

Proper installation for a weather-tight roof.

Installation does not require wet work and curing during construction, allowing for rapid project completion.

Installation considerations

  • Ensure purlins are spaced correctly for the sheet thickness.
  • Pre-drill holes to prevent cracking the sheets.
  • Use appropriate washers and fasteners for weatherproofing.
  • Maintain correct overlaps (side and end laps) for water runoff.

Dadex sales offices and technical support staff are available for installation queries.
