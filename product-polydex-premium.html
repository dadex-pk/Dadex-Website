Product Overview

Engineered for dependable hot and cold water supply.

Polydex Premium uses PP-RCT — polypropylene random copolymer with modified crystallinity and improved temperature resistance. This fourth-generation material offers higher performance at elevated temperatures compared with standard PP-R.

Product at a glance

  • Resistance to corrosion & abrasion
  • Environment friendly
  • Welding capability
  • Low electrical & heat conductivity
