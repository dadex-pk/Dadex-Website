Product Overview

Engineered for dependable hot and cold water supply.

Polydex Premium uses PP-RCT — polypropylene random copolymer with modified crystallinity and improved temperature resistance. This fourth-generation material offers significantly higher performance at elevated temperatures compared with standard PP-R, making it the premium choice for demanding building services.

Application Areas

Where Polydex Premium can be used

  • High-temperature hot water distribution
  • High-pressure cold water systems
  • Commercial and high-rise residential buildings
  • District cooling and heating networks

Final application suitability should be assessed against the system's design conditions and applicable technical requirements.

Installation

Proper installation for dependable performance.

Correct installation practices are essential to the performance and service life of a piping system. Polydex Premium connections are made using heat-fusion jointing.

Installation considerations

  • Use compatible PP-RCT pipes and fittings.
  • Follow the applicable heat-fusion procedure carefully.
  • Allow for thermal expansion in hot water lines.
  • Carry out appropriate pressure testing before commissioning.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
