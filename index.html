Dadex Eternit Limited is a leading manufacturer of thermoplastic piping and roofing systems in Pakistan. Established as a public limited company in 1959, Dadex offers a broad range of solutions for water supply, soil, waste and vent, sewerage and drainage, electrical and cable protection, gas and specialised industrial applications.
