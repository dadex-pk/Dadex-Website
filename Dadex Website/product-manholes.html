PRODUCT OVERVIEW

A complete system for underground sewer access.

The manhole system is built from compatible chamber bases, rising pipes, seals, covers and supporting components to provide access to underground sewerage and drainage networks.

01

Product at a glance

  • Three system types: 315, 400 and 425
  • Complete modular chamber components
  • Multiple cover and frame configurations
  • Designed for sewerage and drainage infrastructure

Technical Specifications

Manholes
System componentsChamber base, rising pipe, chamber top/cover, seals, reducing rings and supporting cover/frame components.
Type 315Uses a corrugated rising pipe system with compatible bases, covers and fittings.
Type 400Uses a smooth rising pipe and can be configured with straight or multi-inlet chamber bases.
Type 425Uses a corrugated rising pipe system with compatible bases, covers and fittings.
Cover optionsThe system documentation identifies cover configurations for pedestrian and traffic areas, including A15, B125 and D400 classes.
StandardsSource documentation identifies EN 13598-2, EN 124 and EN 681-1 requirements for relevant chamber, cover and seal applications.

Complete system

Compatible bases, rising pipes, covers, seals and transition components form a coordinated underground access system.

Durable access

Plastic chamber components provide durable, corrosion-resistant construction for underground environments.

Traffic-area options

The system includes cover options suitable for different pedestrian and traffic loading conditions, subject to the approved configuration.
