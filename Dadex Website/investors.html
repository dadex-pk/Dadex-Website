INVESTOR RELATIONS

Profile of Company

Dadex was established as a public limited company in 1959. The company began with fiber-cement roofing systems in Hyderabad under the leadership of Founder Chairman Kassim Dada. Under the forward-looking leadership of Sikander Dada, the business diversified into plastic piping systems.

Today Dadex manufactures thermoplastic systems including UPVC, PP-R, PP-RCT, PP and PE. Headquartered in Karachi, the company operates manufacturing facilities in Hyderabad and Sundar Industrial Estate near Lahore.

Dadex states that its operations are supported by ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certifications together with PSQCA certification. Its stated focus is customer service, quality, innovation and reliable technical support.

Permissible Business

The principal business of the Company is the manufacture and sale of construction material, mainly including piping systems and allied products manufactured from fiber cement, rubber and plastics, together with merchandising of imported fittings, accessories and other building products.

The wording above reflects the company’s statutory business description. Current product pages on this website should be read as the operational product portfolio rather than as a replacement for the company’s constitutional business description.

Governance / Code of Conduct

Dadex’s governance principles emphasise honesty, integrity, legal and regulatory compliance, fair competition, responsible business conduct, product safety, environmental responsibility, reliable financial reporting, merit-based employment, confidentiality and avoidance/disclosure of conflicts of interest.

  1. Conduct business honestly and ethically, respecting stakeholders.
  2. Comply with Pakistani laws and regulations.
  3. Support fair competition and compliance with competition laws.
  4. Do not support political parties or political-party interests.
  5. Promote legitimate interests through trade associations.
  6. Provide safe products offering value in price and quality.
  7. Operate in an environmentally responsible manner and contribute to society.
  8. Maintain reliable financial reporting and transparency.
  9. Recruit and promote on merit and maintain safe working conditions.
  10. Protect company information and assets and disclose conflicts of interest.
  11. Board committees monitor compliance with these principles.
  12. Management is responsible for enforcement of laws and regulations.
  13. Do not facilitate arrangements involving proceeds of crime.
  14. Treat work information as confidential unless publicly available.
