PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Engineered for dependable compressed air.

WHY T-FLEX

Built for demanding compressed air applications.

Polyethylene construction provides a durable alternative to traditional steel or metal piping, particularly where corrosion and underground conditions are concerns.

Resistant to CorrosionT-Flex pipes do not corrode over time. Premium polyethylene grades resist corrosion and help prevent blockage of air-jet nozzles, providing durability and an extended design life compared with traditional steel or metal pipes.
Resistant to Contaminated Sub-soilsT-Flex pipes do not rust or rot as a result of underground leaching. They remain unaffected when buried in acidic, alkaline, corrosive or saline soils.
Cost-Effective & Maintenance-FreeT-Flex pipes do not require external wrapping or cathodic protection generally needed for metal pipes to prevent corrosion, making them a cost-effective and maintenance-free solution.
Extendable ConnectionsT-Flex provides the provision for flanged connections and can be connected to metallic manifolds above ground.
