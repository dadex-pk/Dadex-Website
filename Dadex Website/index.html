THE DADEX DIFFERENCE

A legacy built on trust.

Dadex Eternit Limited is a leading manufacturer of thermoplastic piping systems and roofing systems. Established as a public limited company in 1959, Dadex has developed a broad range of systems serving water supply, soil waste and vent, sewerage and drainage, electrical and cable, gas and other applications.
