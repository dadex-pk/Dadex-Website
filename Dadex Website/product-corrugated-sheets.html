PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Corrugated Sheets for roofing and wall cladding.

Dadex Corrugated Sheets are used for roofing and wall cladding. They facilitate design flexibility, reduce construction time and increase the efficiency and quality of installation work, thereby resulting in lower project costs. The installation is especially simplified as Corrugated Sheets do not require wet work and curing during construction.

01

Applications

  • Domestic and low cost housing
  • Port structures
  • Industrial, agricultural and commercial buildings
  • Farms, sheds and go downs
  • Warehouses and factories

Advantages

Benefits over conventional roofing materials

Dadex Corrugates sheets have clear advantages over reinforced cement concrete roofs, galvanized iron corrugated sheets and any other roofing material. Dadex Corrugates sheets have the following benefits over conventional material:

Economical, maintenance free, easy to install
Do not corrode as do metal corrugated sheets
Highly resistant to extreme weather conditions
Resistant to industrial smoke, fire, rodents, rotting
Low noise during impact of rain/hail
Provide a watertight seal
Provide insulation
