Product Overview

Reliable building drainage.

Nikasi is a UPVC soil, waste and ventilation system for internal building drainage. It handles waste water, soil discharge and ventilation from bathrooms, kitchens and sanitary installations, and is designed for reliable, low-maintenance service in buildings.

Product at a glance

  • Low flow loss
  • Non-corrosiveness & chemical resistance
  • Light weight
  • Self-extinguishing
