Product Overview

Reliable building drainage.

Nikasi is a dedicated UPVC soil, waste, and ventilation system for internal building drainage. It expertly handles waste water, soil discharge, and ventilation from bathrooms, kitchens, and sanitary installations, ensuring reliable, low-maintenance service in modern buildings.

Application Areas

Where Nikasi can be used

  • Internal sanitary drainage networks
  • Soil and waste discharge systems
  • Ventilation piping
  • Residential and commercial buildings

The smooth bore and non-corrosive material ensure efficient flow and a long service life.

Installation

Proper installation for leak-free drainage.

Whether using solvent cement or rubber ring push-fit joints, proper preparation ensures a durable, watertight building drainage system.

Installation considerations

  • Cut pipes squarely and deburr edges before jointing.
  • Clean surfaces thoroughly when using solvent cement.
  • Ensure rubber rings are seated correctly in the socket.
  • Provide adequate support brackets to prevent pipe sagging.

Refer to the relevant product documentation for detailed installation instructions.
